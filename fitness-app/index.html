Exercise meanings

Tap to read simple explanations
  • Chair squat: Sit down onto a chair, then stand up again. Good for legs and hips.
  • Hip hinge with dumbbells: Hold the weights, keep your back straight, bend a little from the hips, then stand tall again.
  • Shoulder press: Push the dumbbells up from shoulder height, then lower them slowly.
  • One-arm row: Pull one dumbbell upward toward your ribs while leaning forward a little.
  • Floor chest press: Lie on your back and press the dumbbells upward from your chest.
  • Wall push-up: Push yourself away from a wall or counter. Easier than floor push-ups.
  • Farmer walk: Hold weights in your hands and walk slowly with good posture.
  • Calf raise: Lift your heels up, then lower them again.
  • Single-leg stand: Stand on one leg while holding a chair or wall if needed.
  • Heel-to-toe walk: Walk in a straight line, heel touching the toes of the other foot.
  • Hip stretch: Gentle stretch for the front of the hip.
  • Hamstring stretch: Gentle stretch for the back of the thigh.
  • Upper-back twist: Gentle turning of the upper body to keep the back mobile.

Safety

  • Work at a steady pace, not an exhausting one.
  • Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp pain.
  • In hot weather, exercise early or late and drink water.
  • If anything medical changes, check with a doctor before increasing intensity.
